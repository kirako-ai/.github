Kirako designs and deploys AI agents that work with the tools companies already use. We also help people learn to use AI and identify where it makes sense in their work.

### What we do

- [AI agents and automation](https://kirako.ai/services/agents-ia): custom agents connected to business tools and workflows.
- [AI training and coaching](https://kirako.ai/services/formation): practical training for teams and individuals.
- [AI audits and advice](https://kirako.ai/services/conseil): on-site observation and an action plan you can put to work.

Our work spans Hermes, OpenClaw, n8n and Python. We connect agents to existing systems, keep people in control of sensitive actions, and build for day-to-day use.
